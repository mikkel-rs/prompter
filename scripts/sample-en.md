# Opening remarks

Good evening everyone, and thank you for coming. [pause, look at the room]

A year ago the club had twelve active members under forty and a court that sat empty four nights a week. Tonight there are sixty of us. The court is booked until ten. That did not happen by itself, and it did not happen because of the committee. It happened because somebody brought a friend.

I want to spend ten minutes on three things. First, what we learned about getting new players to stay. Second, what it cost us. Third, what we did not solve, because there are still things we did not solve.

# What we learned

New players quit after three weeks if they have nobody to play with. Not three months. Three weeks. So the most important thing we did this year was to pair every new player with two others at their own level inside the first week.

It sounds obvious. It took us five years to work out.

# What it cost

Fees did not go up. We spent the money on balls and on coffee. The coffee was the better investment.
